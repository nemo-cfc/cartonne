Privacy policy

When you request a quote we collect your name, phone number, email, brand name and packaging details. We use them only to prepare your quote and follow up on your order. We do not sell or share your details with third parties, except couriers who need your address to deliver samples.

To ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted, email hello@cartonne.in.

Terms

Quotes are valid for 15 days. Production starts after proof approval and a 50% advance. The balance is due before dispatch. If the final print does not match your signed proof, Cartonne will reprint the affected units at no cost.
