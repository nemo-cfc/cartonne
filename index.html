Questions

Before you ask for a quote

Still unsure? A packaging specialist can answer on the phone in under five minutes.

Call +91 80 4718 2290
What is your minimum order quantity?

250 units for mailer boxes, mono cartons and paper bags. 300 units for rigid boxes. You can split the run across two artwork versions at no extra cost.

How long does production take?

12 to 18 working days after you approve the final proof, plus 2 to 5 days for delivery depending on your city. Rush slots of 8 days are available most months.

Can I see a sample before I order?

Yes. We send a free unprinted structural sample in your exact size within 5 working days, plus a 3D digital proof of your artwork.

Do you help with design and dielines?

Yes. The dieline is free. Our in house designer can also adapt your artwork for print, starting at ₹2,450 per design.

What if the print does not match my proof?

We reprint the affected boxes at our cost. Every job is checked against your signed proof before it leaves the plant.

Are your materials recyclable?

Our kraft and duplex boards are FSC certified and fully recyclable. We print with water based inks and can skip plastic lamination entirely.

How do payments work?

The quote and sample are free. Once you approve the proof, you pay 50% to start production and the balance before dispatch. We accept UPI, NEFT and cards, and issue GST invoices.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We ship to every pin code in India and can split delivery between your warehouse and fulfilment partner.
